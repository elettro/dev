Our Story

Ndless Entertainment was founded with one belief: every artist has a story worth telling — they just need the right team to tell it.

For over 30 years, we've been the behind-the-scenes force for artists, labels, and entertainment professionals across the music industry. We've watched the landscape change — from print to digital, from radio to streaming — and we've evolved every step of the way.

What hasn't changed is our commitment to the artists we serve. We put your vision, your brand, and your long-term career first. Always have. Always will.

PR campaigns. Press releases. Artist bios. EPKs. Social media marketing. Consultations. Six core offerings built on three decades of experience.

Common Questions
FAQ

Do you work with artists at any career stage?

Absolutely. We work with first-time artists recording their debut single all the way to established industry veterans. Our approach is tailored to wherever you are.

What does the free consultation include?

A real, no-pressure conversation about your goals, your current situation, and how we can help. No sales pitch — just honest advice from 30 years of industry experience.

Can I hire you for just one service?

Yes. Whether you need a single press release or a full campaign rollout, we work with you on the scope that fits your needs and your budget.

How long does a typical campaign take?

It depends on the scope. A press release can be turned around in days. A full PR campaign typically runs 4–12 weeks. We'll set clear timelines during the strategy phase.
