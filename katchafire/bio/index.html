Whakapapa · Origin

It started with a father and his two teenage sons moving into an apartment in Hamilton — a place where the music could run all night long. Lead guitarist Grenville Bell, his son Logan on vocals, and Jordan behind the drums laid the foundation of what would become a global reggae phenomenon.
